# Playbook

The owner's taste, distilled from feedback. Read before pitching and before every version. Add a line when feedback is a general rule, not a one-off fix; name the date and the reel. The starting rules below come from experience with short vertical videos; the owner's feedback overrides them.

## Hook and pacing
- The hook is 3–4 s at most and moves from the very first frame.
- The first frame doubles as the cover: a clean composition with the headline in the middle or lower part, never at the top.
- Scene changes land on bar starts of the track's beat grid.
- Total length 15–20 s.

## Story
- Show what the product really does today, on its real screens.
- A real, emotional pain beats a neutral example.
- One headline per scene; on-screen text never repeats itself.
- Show a feature as a run of steps, not a single one.

## Look
- Every reel gets its own palette, type and transitions, designed from its subject.
- Push the camera in on the part of the screen that matters instead of showing the whole phone.
- No pulsing on beats, no shake; camera moves are slow eased push-ins and pans.
- No moving decorative text in the background.

## Music
- Playful and upbeat by default, never plaintive.
- Every reel gets its own track. Used or rejected tracks are listed here and never offered again.
- Loudness about −15 LUFS, peak below −1 dBFS; trap tracks need a limiter after loudnorm.
